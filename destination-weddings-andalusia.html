Destination wedding flamenco: frequently asked questions

Can you perform at a villa, hotel, finca or other private wedding venue?

We consider private venues throughout Andalusia, subject to availability, suitable performance conditions and the practical arrangements agreed with the venue.

Can you adapt the performance to the ceremony, cocktail hour or evening party?

Yes. We discuss the running order and recommend solo guitar, Son del Alma or Suite Flamenca depending on the moment and atmosphere you want to create.

Do you coordinate with our wedding planner?

Yes. We can discuss access, timing, sound and flooring with your planner or venue team while remaining responsible for the agreed artistic service.

Are sound, lighting, travel and staging always included?

No. Technical equipment, staging and travel requirements depend on the venue and event. Any applicable items are specified in the bespoke quotation.

How can we check availability and receive a quote?

Contact Azahara with your intended date, venue and preferred musical format. We discuss the details personally before sending a quotation; an enquiry does not confirm a booking.
