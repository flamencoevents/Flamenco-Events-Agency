Flamenco for Weddings & Events: Questions and Answers

15 questions

What flamenco entertainment can we book?
We offer solo flamenco guitar, Son del Alma (guitar and voice) and Suite Flamenca (live guitar, singing and dance). We discuss the best lineup for your occasion before preparing a quotation.
Is live flamenco suitable for a wedding ceremony?
Yes. Solo guitar or guitar and voice can accompany a ceremony or guest arrival, subject to the venue, repertoire and schedule agreed in advance.
Can you perform during cocktails or dinner?
Yes. We can discuss guitar or guitar-and-voice performances for cocktails and dinners, or a dedicated flamenco dance performance at a suitable moment.
Can we book flamenco dancers with live musicians?
Yes. Suite Flamenca combines live flamenco music and dance. The final number of artists and performance structure are agreed for each event.
Can the performance include several sets?
We can plan several performance segments when suitable. Their number, duration and any costume changes must be agreed and included in the quotation.
Do you work with international wedding planners?
Yes. We coordinate the artistic performance with the wedding planner and venue team. We are the entertainment provider, not the organiser of the entire wedding.
Where in Spain can you perform?
We receive enquiries for Marbella, Málaga, Seville, Granada and other locations in Spain. Availability and any travel arrangements depend on the event.
Can you perform at a hotel, private villa or finca?
We can consider hotels, villas, estates and other event venues if the performance conditions, access and technical requirements are suitable.
What technical requirements should we plan for?
We discuss the performing area, electrical access, sound requirements, safe dance flooring, weather contingencies and installation times with your venue or planner.
Do you bring sound, lighting or a dance floor?
Sound, lighting and any portable dance floor or staging are assessed separately. They are included only when expressly listed in your quotation.
How do I request a quotation?
Contact Azahara with your intended date, venue, event type and preferred performance. She speaks with you before preparing a tailored quotation.
Can I check availability before booking?
Yes. Send the date, location and preferred format so we can review your enquiry. Asking about availability does not reserve the date.
What does the quotation include?
The written quotation identifies the agreed artistic services, performance schedule and any applicable technical, travel or other costs. Confirm the exact scope before booking.
Are travel costs included in the price?
Travel and accommodation needs vary by location and artist lineup. Any applicable costs are shown in the bespoke quotation.
Can we tailor the repertoire and artistic format?
We can discuss repertoire and performance format to match the occasion. Final feasibility depends on the musicians, lead time and requirements of the event.
